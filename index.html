02 · About

A little about me

I'm a software engineer who enjoys the unglamorous parts of systems: the retry logic, the dead-letter queue, the deployment pipeline that means nobody gets paged at 2 a.m. For 2+ years I've shipped production Spring Boot microservices and cloud-native systems on AWS and Azure.

At NEC Software Solutions I helped break legacy monoliths into a dozen-plus microservices and built self-healing error-handling workflows. More recently, during my Master's at Arizona State University, I've worked on AI interpretability research for a thesis project in human-centered AI, using sparse autoencoders to help people understand why a model fails. I also co-authored a systematic review on robot-learning data collection, now under review at HRI 2027.

I'm an AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate, and I like building products that help people. Recent examples are an emotional-wellness platform and an accessibility assistant for people who are visually impaired.
